Do's and Don'ts for responsible social media use as a college student:

Do’s:
> As a rule of thumb, it’s always best to avoid controversial topics. 
> Consider the potential impact of your posts before sharing them.
> Use social media platforms to showcase your achievements, skills, and interests related to your field of study or desired career.
> Make your profiles private, if that’s an option.
> Do watch out for fake friend requests.



Don’ts:
> Be negative or aggressive online. Free speech is important and you are entitled to you but be smart about it. Don’t be a hater and don’t be a troll. Hateful, harmful and hurtful comments will not paint a positive picture of you. 
> Share sexually explicit stuff. That can come back to haunt you in the long run.
> Rely on privacy settings. They aren’t always as reliable as you might think them to be.
> Post anything illegal. That means drugs, violence, or any sort of stealing or damaging of property!
> Spending too much time on social media can negatively impact your studies. With great time management skills, you can build an online presence while also making time to study, participate in extracurricular activities.
